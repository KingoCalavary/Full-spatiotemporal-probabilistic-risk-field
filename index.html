Methodological Comparison Summary:
  • Variance Inflation: Represents the vehicle as an enlarged ellipse. It ignores rigid boundaries and significantly underestimates risk at corners.
  • Sampling Approximation: Performs mathematical convolution over the vehicle footprint. Notice that as uncertainties increase, it suffers from plateau collapse (the peak intensity drops below 1.0) and blurry edges.
  • Proposed Set-based: Transforms the overlap integral into a geometric distance in a whitened space. It ensures physical consistency by maintaining a constant RFI of 1.0 strictly within the expanded physical boundaries, regardless of the uncertainty magnitude.
